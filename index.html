XI Fantasy Picks Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

The short version
  • XI Fantasy Picks is a private app for fantasy football leagues among friends: weekly pick'em, a leaderboard, standings and league news.
  • It collects only what it needs to run your league.
  • Nothing is sold. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time.

Who runs it

XI Fantasy Picks is made by an independent developer in the United States. Questions or requests: xideveloping@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your email address, to sign you in. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose "Hide My Email", we get Apple's private relay address instead.
  • Your profile in each league: the name you enter, an optional nickname, an optional profile photo and team logo you upload, your team crest, and your role (admin or member).
  • Your league activity: your weekly picks, results and wins, and the dues and payouts your league admins record. The app only keeps a record; no money moves through it.
  • League notes that admins post.
  • ESPN league data for leagues connected to ESPN: teams, owner names as ESPN shows them, records, matchups, scores, roster moves and team logos.
  • The saved ESPN login. For a private ESPN league, one member logs in to ESPN in the app. We keep that member's ESPN session cookies, stored encrypted on our server and used only to read that league from ESPN. Your ESPN password is never seen or stored by the app.
  • Reports and blocks: reports you file (what you reported and your optional reason) and the people you block.
  • Push notification tokens, if you allow notifications, so the app can send league reminders.
  • On your device: your sign-in session, stored encrypted.

We don't collect your location, contacts, browsing history, device identifiers for advertising, or payment information.

How it's used

Only to run your league in the app: showing picks, scores, the leaderboard, standings and news. Everything in a league is visible to that league's members. Other leagues can't see it.

The AI podcast

Some leagues have an AI-generated podcast. It's off by default and turned on per league by the developer. For those leagues only, league content (members' names and nicknames, team names, scores, standings and results) is sent to Google's Gemini API to write and voice each episode. The podcast uses Gemini's free tier, and Google may use data sent through it to improve its products, under Google's terms. Episode audio is kept for 6 weeks; the transcript stays with the league.

Who processes it

We use these service providers to run the app. None of them buys your data, and we don't share it for advertising.

  • Supabase: database, sign-in, file storage and server functions, hosted in the western United States.
  • Expo: app updates and push notification delivery.
  • Google: Gemini, for podcast leagues only; Gmail, to send sign-in links and report emails.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, if you use it.
  • ESPN: the source of league data for connected leagues.

Reports and blocking

You can report another member's photo, nickname or team logo, or a league note. Reports go to your league's admins in the app and to the developer by email. The person you report is never told who reported them. Blocking someone hides their photo, nickname, team logo and notes from you only; they stay in the league's picks, leaderboard and standings, and they aren't told.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to League → You → Delete account. If you haven't joined a league yet, it's on the Welcome screen: Delete account.

  • Removed: your sign-in and email, nickname, profile photo and uploaded team logo; the ESPN login you saved for a league, if any; and any league where you're the only member. If you used Sign in with Apple, the app is removed from Sign in with Apple in your Apple ID settings.
  • Stays: past results, standings and payouts, so your league's history doesn't change. Your name there is shown as "Former member". Recaps and podcast episodes already published aren't rewritten.

The only admin of a league with other members needs to make someone else admin first. You can also ask the developer to delete your account at xideveloping@gmail.com.

How long we keep it

As long as your account exists, except podcast audio, which is deleted after 6 weeks. Our hosting provider's backups may hold deleted data for a limited time before it's overwritten.

Children

XI Fantasy Picks isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too, and important changes are announced in the app.
